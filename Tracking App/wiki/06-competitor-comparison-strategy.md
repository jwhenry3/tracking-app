## Differentiating Growth Strategies

### 1. Vs. Microsoft Teams: Avoid Enterprise Friction & Overhead
* **Where Teams dominates:** Enterprise corporate IT, heavy SharePoint document workflows, and corporate video calls.
* **Our Edge:** Zero corporate overhead. No complex Azure AD setup, no meeting fatigue. Mobile-first, instant 5-second logging for calendars, budgets, and tasks for everyday life and micro-teams.

### 2. Vs. Slack: Avoid Per-Seat Software Taxes & Data Caps
* **Where Slack dominates:** B2B developer integrations and enterprise threads.
* **Our Edge:** Slack enforces $7.25–$8.75/user/month and hides message history after 90 days on free plans. We offer **unlimited permanent message history** on the free tier and a **flat $11.99/mo workspace pass** with no seat penalties.

### 3. Vs. Discord: Eliminate Noise & Real-Time Distractions
* **Where Discord dominates:** Live voice lounges, gaming streams, and massive public communities.
* **Our Edge:** Discord is noisy and unorganized for real-world life logistics. We offer structured, quiet tools specifically designed to track money, tasks, and schedules.
