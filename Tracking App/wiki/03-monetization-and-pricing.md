# Monetization & Adjusted Pricing Strategy

## Monetization Philosophy
The pricing architecture is designed to undercut incumbents (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord) by replacing aggressive **per-seat software taxes** with predictable **workspace flat-rates**.

---

## Adjusted 3-Tier Pricing Comparison Matrix

| Feature / Limit | Free Starter Pass | Pro User Pass | Pro Workspace Pass |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | **$0 / month forever** | **$4.99 / mo** (or $39/yr) | **$11.99 / workspace / mo** (or $99/yr) |
| **Target Audience** | Casual users & group onboarding | Individuals, couples, power users | Families, roommates, small teams |
| **Active Workspaces** | Up to 2 Workspaces | Up to 6 Workspaces | Unlimited Workspaces |
| **Members per Workspace** | Max 5 Members | Max 10 Members | **Unlimited Members (No seat tax)** |
| **Message History** | **Unlimited & Permanent** | **Unlimited & Permanent** | **Unlimited & Permanent** |
| **Unified Calendar** | Standard View | **Privacy Masking Enabled** | **Privacy Masking Enabled** |
| **Finance Tracking** | Up to 30 entries / month | **Unlimited Personal Ledger** | **Unlimited Shared Ledger + Exports** |
| **Storage** | 500 MB Shared | 10 GB Total | 50 GB per Workspace |
