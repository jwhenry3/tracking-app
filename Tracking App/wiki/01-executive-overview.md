# Executive Overview & Application Architecture

## Product Vision
The goal of this application is to serve as a unified **Group Operating System**�a single workspace platform combining **scheduling, task/home planning, manual financial tracking, and group chat**. 

By serving both personal life organization and group/workspace collaboration within the same interface, it eliminates app fragmentation and replaces multiple single-purpose subscription tools.

---

## Core Product Pillars

1. **Agnostic Workspaces:** Workspaces are structurally identical containers ("servers"). Whether used for personal task management, family life, roommates, or a small business, every workspace possesses the exact same feature set and governance primitive.
2. **Unified Personal Aggregation:** While workspaces store isolated data, individual user accounts aggregate events and financial logs across joined workspaces into a single pane of glass (Unified Calendar and Master Financial Feed).
3. **Manual Financial Clarity & Privacy:** Finance tracking requires zero bank linking, emphasizing privacy, intentional spending, manual input speed, and shared accountability.
4. **Discord-Style Workspace Governance:** Each workspace maintains its own channels, member roster, invite links, and permission defaults.

---

## High-Level Value Proposition
* **For Families & Roommates:** Replaces fragmented group text threads, wall calendars, and bill-splitting apps with one organized household hub.
* **For Small Businesses & Teams:** Eliminates high per-seat software taxes (Slack, Asana, YNAB) with flat-rate, all-in-one team collaboration.
* **For Individuals:** Provides clean separation of life domains (Work, Personal, Side Hustle, Family) without logging in and out of different apps.
